 From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia 
 American artificial intelligence engineer 
 Kyle Kosic is an American artificial intelligence engineer.
 Kosic worked at OnScale as a software engineer and as a quantitative analyst at Lucena Research. [ 1 ] In 2021, he joined OpenAI . [ 2 ] In May 2023, he joined xAI . In June 2024, he left xAI and returned to OpenAI where he worked on the technical staff. [ 3 ] [ 1 ] In 2026, he joined Project Prometheus , a start-up founded by Jeff Bezos in 2025 which is focused on the use of AI in the physical world including industrial applications . [ 4 ]
